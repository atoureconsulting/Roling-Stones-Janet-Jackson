# Commission structure

Source of truth for the generated PDFs: commission structure and sponsorship
team briefing, each in English and French.

Project: Janet Jackson in Côte d'Ivoire, Culture on Culture, Rolling Stone Africa.

---

## How AToure is paid

| | What | Basis | Who shares it |
|---|---|---|---|
| **35%** | Agency fee | Of the total tour budget, charged on top of it | AToure 30%. KalyEvents 5%. |
| **30%** | Sponsorship commission | Of every sponsor brought to the project | Depends on who brings the sponsor |
| **$1,000** | Day rate | For every day on the ground in Côte d'Ivoire | AToure only |
| **At cost** | Costs on the ground | Reimbursed in full, on top of everything above | Against receipts and written justification |

---

## One. Agency fee, 35%

Charged on top of the budget, not taken out of it.

| If the tour budget is | Total fee 35% | KalyEvents 5% | AToure 30% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 57,957,000 base estimated total | 20,284,950 | 2,897,850 | 17,387,100 |
| 65,000,000 working envelope low | 22,750,000 | 3,250,000 | 19,500,000 |
| 70,000,000 working envelope high | 24,500,000 | 3,500,000 | 21,000,000 |

### Why the split sits where it does

1. **We engage the teams.** AToure appoints, briefs and pays everyone who
   delivers on the ground.
2. **Our name carries it.** Every commitment made to Rolling Stone Africa, to
   the institutions and to the sponsors stands on the AToure reputation.
3. **We carry the overheads.** Staffing, coordination, transport, security
   liaison, permits, and the work before and after the visit.

KalyEvents is paid for the introduction. AToure is paid for the delivery.

**Advance.** AToure requests an advance on the agency fee before delivery
begins. Amount and timing to be agreed.

## Two. Sponsorship, 30%

**AToure brings the sponsor.** AToure 20%, KalyEvents 10%, sponsorship team nothing.

**The sponsorship team brings the sponsor.** AToure 18%, KalyEvents 7%,
sponsorship team 5%.

Both AToure and KalyEvents give up points so the sponsorship team is paid on
what it brings. AToure gives up two, KalyEvents gives up three.

| Sponsor value | Commission 30% | AToure | KalyEvents | Sponsorship team |

| 100,000,000 | 30,000,000 | 20,000,000 / 18,000,000 | 10,000,000 / 7,000,000 | 0 / 5,000,000 |

First figure is case one, second is case two.

## Three. Day rate, $1,000

For every day on the ground in Côte d'Ivoire. Billed to Rolling Stone Africa on
top of the agency fee. Not shared.

| Days on the ground | Total USD | Total FCFA |
|---|---|---|
| 5 days | $5,000 | 2,835,000 |
| 7 days | $7,000 | 3,969,000 |
| 10 days | $10,000 | 5,670,000 |

Seven days is the programme currently planned.

## Four. Costs, reimbursed in full

Every cost AToure incurs during the tour is reimbursed in full, separately from
the agency fee and the day rate.

1. AToure provides a receipt for every item.
2. Each item carries a written justification of what it was for.
3. Costs are submitted as one schedule, not as running requests.

---

## Every approach is made in the name of AToure

1. Every approach to a company is made on AToure letterhead.
2. Anyone contacting a company does so for AToure, never in their own name.
3. AToure takes part at every stage: decision making, pitching and sign off.
4. Nothing is sent, agreed or signed without AToure.

Sponsors are registered before contact: the prospect name goes to AToure first,
AToure confirms in writing that it is registered to that person, and that
confirmation is what secures the five points. First written registration wins.
