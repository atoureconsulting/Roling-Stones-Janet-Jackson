# Commission structure, internal

Confidential. Internal use only. Source of truth for the generated PDFs:
commission structure and sponsorship team briefing, each in English and French.

Project: Janet Jackson in Côte d'Ivoire, Culture on Culture, Rolling Stone Africa.

---

## Three revenue streams

| Stream | Rate | Calculated on | Divides between |
|---|---|---|---|
| One. Agency fee | 35% | The total tour budget, charged on top | AToure and KalyEvents |
| Two. Sponsorship | 30% | Each sponsor brought to the project | Depends on who brings the sponsor |
| Three. Day rate | $1,000 | Each day on the ground in Côte d'Ivoire | AToure |

---

## Stream one: agency fee

35% of the total tour budget, charged **on top of the budget**, not carved out.

1. KalyEvents: 5 points
2. AToure: 30 points

As a share of the fee itself, AToure takes 85.7% and KalyEvents takes 14.3%.

| If the tour budget is | Total fee 35% | KalyEvents 5% | AToure 30% |
|---|---|---|---|
| 57,957,000 base estimated total | 20,284,950 | 2,897,850 | 17,387,100 |
| 65,000,000 working envelope low | 22,750,000 | 3,250,000 | 19,500,000 |
| 70,000,000 working envelope high | 24,500,000 | 3,500,000 | 21,000,000 |

### Why AToure retains thirty of the thirty five

1. **We engage the teams.** AToure appoints, briefs and pays the people who
   deliver the project on the ground.
2. **Our name carries the project.** Every commitment made to Rolling Stone
   Africa, to the institutions and to the sponsors stands on the AToure
   reputation. If anything goes wrong, it is our name that answers for it.
3. **We carry the overheads.** Staffing, coordination, transport, security
   liaison, permits and the considerable work before and after the visit.

KalyEvents is compensated for the introduction. AToure is compensated for the
delivery.

### Advance on the agency fee

AToure will request an advance on the agency fee, payable before delivery
begins, so that setup costs are not carried out of pocket. The amount and the
timing are to be agreed with Rolling Stone Africa.

## Stream two: sponsorship

30% commission on every sponsor brought to the project.

**Case one. AToure brings the sponsor.** AToure 20, KalyEvents 10, sponsorship
team nothing.

**Case two. The sponsorship team brings the sponsor.** AToure 18, KalyEvents 7,
sponsorship team 5.

Both AToure and KalyEvents give up points so that the sponsorship team is paid
on what it brings. AToure gives up two points, KalyEvents gives up three.

| Sponsor value | Commission 30% | AToure 20 or 18 | KalyEvents 10 or 7 | Team 0 or 5 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 10,000,000 | 3,000,000 | 2,000,000 or 1,800,000 | 1,000,000 or 700,000 | 0 or 500,000 |
| 25,000,000 | 7,500,000 | 5,000,000 or 4,500,000 | 2,500,000 or 1,750,000 | 0 or 1,250,000 |

| 100,000,000 | 30,000,000 | 20,000,000 or 18,000,000 | 10,000,000 or 7,000,000 | 0 or 5,000,000 |

First figure is case one, second is case two.

## Stream three: day rate

$1,000 for every day the team is on the ground in Côte d'Ivoire delivering the
project. Billed to Rolling Stone Africa in addition to the agency fee, and not
shared with any other party.

| Days on the ground | Total USD | Total FCFA |
|---|---|---|
| 5 days | $5,000 | 2,835,000 |
| 7 days | $7,000 | 3,969,000 |
| 10 days | $10,000 | 5,670,000 |

The seven day programme is the one currently planned.

## Every approach is made in the name of AToure

1. Every approach to a company is made on AToure letterhead.
2. Anyone contacting a company does so as a representative of AToure, or on
   behalf of AToure, never in their own name.
3. AToure takes part at every stage: decision making, pitching and final sign off.
4. No proposal is sent, no terms are discussed and nothing is signed without AToure.
5. It is the name, the reputation and the relationships of AToure that are on the line.

---

## Registering a sponsor

1. The prospect name goes to AToure before any contact.
2. AToure confirms in writing that the prospect is registered to that person.
3. That written confirmation is what secures the five points.
4. The approach happens only after confirmation, on AToure letterhead.
5. AToure joins the pitch and the sign off.

First written registration wins. Verbal claims after a signature are not accepted.
