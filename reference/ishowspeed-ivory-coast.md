# IShowSpeed x Ivory Coast, 2024

Source: atoureconsulting/website, ivory-coast.html and our-work.html. Pulled August 14, 2026.

## Summary

Brand partners: MTN, Solibra, Snedai. Creator: IShowSpeed. Market: Ivory Coast. AToure built the entire operational and commercial infrastructure from zero: local sponsors, state level facilitation, security, cultural programming and logistics, for a visit with no existing local network to build on.

## Headline figures

1. Instagram views: 55.8M
2. YouTube views: 10M
3. State visas secured: 15
4. Security personnel mobilized: 600 plus
5. Local sponsors sourced and closed from scratch: named on the detailed case study page as MTN, Solibra and Snedai, three in total. Flagging one inconsistency: the our-work.html summary tile for this project says "2 Sponsors placed" instead of 3. Worth reconciling before either number goes in front of Rolling Stone Africa.
6. Presidential recognition: Baba Touré was personally endorsed by the Ivory Coast Minister of Tourism for the activation

## What AToure delivered

1. Identified, negotiated and closed MTN, Solibra and Snedai as official local sponsors from zero pre existing relationships
2. Secured 15 state sponsored visas and border clearances for the full touring team
3. Obtained equipment, filming and broadcast permits across all Ivory Coast jurisdictions
4. Mobilized 600 plus security personnel citywide for mass public gatherings
5. Mobilized Miss Ivory Coast, Magic System and Afro B, and coordinated the Zaouli dance activation that reached 55.8M Instagram views
6. Handled all ground transport, accommodation, hospitality, drone deployment and full live stream production

## The result

The Zaouli cultural activation became, per the site, one of the most shared creator moments in West African digital history. The site describes this project as AToure's most complete demonstration of ground up operational capability: building full commercial, logistical and creative infrastructure in a new market with zero pre existing relationships, and reaching the President's office.
