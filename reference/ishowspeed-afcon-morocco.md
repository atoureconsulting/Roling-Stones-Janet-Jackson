# IShowSpeed x AFCON 2025, Morocco

Source: atoureconsulting/website, ishowspeed-afcon.html and our-work.html. Pulled August 14, 2026.

## Summary

Brand: CAF (Confederation of African Football) and FRMF (Royal Moroccan Football Federation). Creator: IShowSpeed. Market: Morocco. AToure conceived, negotiated and delivered the integration of IShowSpeed into the AFCON 2025 Final as the tournament's official mascot, "Assad" the lion.

## Headline figures

1. Live stream views: 16M. Ranked number 1 most watched live of Speed's entire Africa tour, and number 10 most popular broadcast of his entire career.
2. Subscriber growth: plus 300K in 24 hours
3. Tournament digital impressions driven by the moment: 6.1 billion, described on the site as an AFCON record
4. Tournament video views driven by the moment: 5.2 billion
5. Final match TV audience: 97.3 million
6. Tournament cumulative viewership, TV and digital combined: 3.45 billion
7. Number 1 trending in Morocco and Senegal

## What AToure did

1. Conceived the mascot concept and secured CAF and FRMF sign off
2. Secured state sponsored visas, border clearances and private jet clearances for the full team, plus VIP pickup from the private terminal in Rabat
3. Obtained filming, equipment and drone permits across Morocco
4. Arranged private and state security mobilization from Casablanca through to the Final in Rabat
5. Placed the full team at the top hotel in Rabat and managed hospitality, transport and logistics through the Final
6. Secured Speed's entry to the Final and coordinated the live mascot reveal

## Verification note

The site states these figures are verified from official broadcast and platform data: YouTube Analytics, a CAF report, and a media audit.
