Source: atoureconsulting/website, ashton-hall-africa-tour.html and our-work.html. Pulled August 14, 2026.

## Summary

AToure's largest single creator mandate at the time the case study was published. Five African countries, delivered country by country: Ghana (Accra), Cameroon (Douala), Nigeria (Lagos), Benin (Cotonou), Rwanda (Kigali). Listed on the site as a featured mandate, delivered 2026.

## Country by country

**Ghana, Accra.** Opening leg. Featured post 26.1M views, 1.9M likes: the Ashton Hall and Ashton Small street collab that carried across both audiences, 50.1M views on the collab overall.

**Cameroon, Douala.** Featured post "The Market Lift," 44.3M views across two feed posts, top post 32.5M views and 1.4M likes. Companion post from @ashton_small01: 11.8M views, 506K likes.

**Nigeria, Lagos.** Featured post "The Stadium Run," 13M views, 548K likes. @indian_ashtonhall joined the tour here.

**Benin, Cotonou.** Featured post "The Sack Lift," 31.9M views, 1.4M likes. Highest engagement per view of any leg on the tour. @indian_ashtonhall Benin arrival collab: 2.5M likes, 89.3K sends.

**Rwanda, Kigali.** Featured post "Day 14" (a face mask morning routine), 171M views, the single largest post of the entire tour, 7.5M likes, 633K sends. Other Rwanda posts: Ankole cattle post 56.7M views, "God is amazing" night post 35.1M views, @ashton_small01 "400K Road to 1M" G Wagon post 5.5M likes and 718K sends.
