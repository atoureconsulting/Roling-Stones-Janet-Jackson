# Janet Jackson in Côte d'Ivoire

Project workspace for AToure Management & Consulting's engagement on Rolling Stone Africa's Culture on Culture initiative, bringing Janet Jackson to Côte d'Ivoire for a cultural immersion trip and a LEGEND cover story.

## Status

Nothing is confirmed yet. Rolling Stone Africa sent AToure a concept deck, and a call has since been held to clarify next steps. A call with the Rolling Stone Africa representative is the required next step before any commitments are made, since Rolling Stone Africa is the party organizing the project.

## What the original deck actually is

The deck AToure received is Rolling Stone Africa's own budget projection for the full tour. It covers costs that sit on Janet Jackson's side directly, such as makeup artists and assistants. It is not a proposal for AToure's fee and should not be read as one.

## AToure fee structure

Two separate revenue streams, both still to be finalized with Rolling Stone Africa.

1. Base organization fee. Preferred structure is a percentage of the finalized total tour budget, targeted around twenty five percent, covering activation on the ground in Côte d'Ivoire: sourcing and coordinating the experience, logistics, and the local contacts needed to deliver the trip. If the finalized tour budget turns out too low for a percentage to make sense, this falls back to a flat fee instead. The actual number depends entirely on the tour budget Rolling Stone Africa confirms.
2. Sponsorship commission. A percentage of any sponsorship revenue AToure brings into the project, separate from the base fee above. Percentage still to be set.

## Key parties

1. Rolling Stone Africa. Organizing the project overall, owns the deck and the relationship with Janet Jackson's team.
2. Janet Jackson's team. Subject of the documentary and cover story. Costs for her side, such as makeup and assistants, sit inside Rolling Stone Africa's own budget, not AToure's.
3. Royal House of Sanwi, Krindjabo. Historical tie referenced throughout the deck via Michael Jackson's 1992 visit. Not yet confirmed as participating in this project.
4. AToure Management & Consulting. Engaged as project and tour manager on the ground in Côte d'Ivoire, pending final scope confirmation.
